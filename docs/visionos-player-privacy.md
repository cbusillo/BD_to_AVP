# Shiny 3D Cinema Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Shiny 3D Cinema is a movie player for Apple Vision Pro provided by Shiny
Computers Leasing LLC. This policy covers the Vision Pro player.

## Your movies and library

The player processes movies you select on your device or in a Location you add.
We do not receive your movies, library, or playback history on our servers.
The player has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or account
registration.

The app stores movie names, formats, saved file access, and playback positions
locally so it can reopen your movies and resume playback. It reads file metadata
to detect unavailable or changed sources. The system file picker and any file
provider you choose control access to files outside the app.

For each Location you add, such as a network share, the app stores the folder's
name, saved access to that folder, and the names, sizes and formats of the movie
files it finds there, locally, so it does not have to read every file again.

## Retention and your controls

Library records and saved playback positions remain on your device. Removing a movie
from the library removes its library entry and saved file access; it does not
delete the original video. Removing a Location removes its saved folder access
and file list; it does not change the share. Playback positions are stored in a bounded local
history and may remain until overwritten, removed when that history reaches its
limit, or the app's local data is erased.

## TestFlight, feedback, and links

If you use the TestFlight beta, Apple processes beta activity and diagnostics
under its [TestFlight terms and privacy information](https://testflight.apple.com/).
Apple may make testing statistics and crash reports available to us. Feedback
you send through TestFlight or email includes the content and contact details
you choose to provide, and may include screenshots or diagnostics.

We use feedback to investigate reported issues and improve the app, and retain
it while needed for that work and beta support. Contact us to request deletion
of feedback we hold. Apple and your email provider may retain their own records
under their respective policies. Do not include private movie content or other
sensitive information that is unnecessary to explain an issue.

Opening a web link uses your browser and the destination website. Those services
handle the information needed to load the page under their own privacy policies.
