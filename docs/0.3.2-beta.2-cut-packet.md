- PR #592 makes folder conversion a direct **Add Folder of Movies…** action.

  and **4K Upscale**, with **Smaller File** mapped to Compact guided quality.

  navigation without the redundant outcome pane.

  distinct titles from the same source.

to the corrected Conversion Setup.
