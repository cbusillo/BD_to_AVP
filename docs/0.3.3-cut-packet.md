## Release-Note Seed

Stable 0.3.3 brings the queue workspace, queue editing and scheduling controls,
storage forecasting, completion notifications, and Movie Sharing from the
0.3.3 beta line. It improves conversion stall feedback and recovery, damaged
3D-frame handling, and long conversions while the display sleeps. Movie
Sharing works with Shiny 3D Cinema 0.1.0 (3) on Vision Pro; the player remains
distributed through TestFlight. Completed movies must be converted before
sharing; this release does not promise direct ISO/BDMV playback.

The Mac app requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later. MakeMKV is installed
separately for Blu-ray conversion. See the
[Movie Sharing guide](movie-sharing-beta.md) for pairing and playback setup.
