## Reviewed Release-Note Seed

> BD_to_AVP `v0.3.0-rc.3` hardens PGS subtitle recovery and makes subtitle
> outcomes easier to diagnose. Malformed segments and image data are isolated
> without unbounded decoding, valid subtitle tracks can survive corrupt peers,
> and the app records aggregate subtitle progress in diagnostics while reporting
> partial-output warnings without exposing disc titles or paths. Profile save is
> also directly reachable by VoiceOver and other accessibility tools.
