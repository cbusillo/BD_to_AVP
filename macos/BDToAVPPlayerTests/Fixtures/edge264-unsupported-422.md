`edge264-unsupported-422.264` is upstream edge264-mvc's synthetic
[`overrun_reference_422.264`](https://github.com/jens-duttke/edge264-mvc/blob/f181792e842ce4e99e2ef4a41bfd30c1c1514b42/tests/conformance/2d-synthetic/overrun_reference_422.264),
unchanged, under the BSD-3-Clause license in `macos/Vendor/Edge264MVC-notices/LICENSE_BSD.txt`.
It contains 8-bit 4:2:2 frames. The decoder test verifies that the NV12 consumer
receives no frames and the wrapper reports an unsupported format.
